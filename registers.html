Operational registers

Record decisions before memory becomes the record.

These draft registers support consistent governance across projects, risks and conflicts of interest. Each register stores entries only in the browser on the device being used and can export a CSV backup.

Privacy warning: do not enter beneficiary identity documents, medical details, exact private addresses or other sensitive personal information in these browser-based registers.
