Australian Registration Roadmap

A preparation checklist based on current official guidance. It is not legal or tax advice and does not replace advice specific to the final entity, fundraising locations or overseas activities.

Key dependency: the legal structure must be chosen before the final governing document and ABN/charity-registration path can be completed.
Checking admin access…
01

Choose the legal structure

Compare an incorporated association, company limited by guarantee or another suitable structure against intended scale, governance, liability, grants and whether operations may extend across jurisdictions or overseas.

Decision required from HSKRC: do not finalise a constitution or registration application until this choice is made.
02

Prepare the governing document

The constitution, rules or trust deed must match the selected legal structure and the organisation's actual charitable purposes, not-for-profit operation and governance arrangements.

03

Confirm responsible people

Identify the real directors, committee members or trustees required by the selected structure, confirm consent and eligibility, and record formal appointment decisions.

04

Incorporate or establish the entity

Complete the relevant state/territory or ASIC process for the chosen structure before using the entity as though registration is complete.

05

Apply for the correct ABN

The ABN entity type must match the organisation's legal structure. Keep the legal name consistent across incorporation, the ABR and governing documents.

06

Prepare the ACNC application

Current ACNC guidance expects an active ABN, legal structure, governing document, Responsible People details, operating locations, activities, charity subtypes, financial/governance information and relevant tax/DGR information.

07

Address overseas-operation controls

Because the proposed humanitarian model includes activities outside Australia, assess the ACNC External Conduct Standards and ensure overseas governance, partner, financial and safeguarding controls are aligned before operations commence.

08

Assess DGR eligibility separately

Charity registration does not automatically mean donations are tax deductible. DGR eligibility depends on the applicable category and endorsement requirements.

09

Check fundraising registration where funds will be solicited

Fundraising regulation can depend on the state or territory. For example, Victoria uses the Consumer Affairs Victoria fundraiser framework and current guidance says applications should be lodged before fundraising begins.

10

Open organisation banking and activate operations only after verification

After the entity and authorised people are established, open separate banking, confirm payment controls, insurance and any required registrations. Only then should donation functionality move from pre-launch to active.
