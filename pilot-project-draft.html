Internal pilot design

Afghanistan Winter & Food Relief Pilot

Project code: HSKRC-PILOT-001. This is a private planning record only. It is not approved for fundraising, partner engagement, expenditure or delivery.

Current gate: Governance approval pending. No local partner has been selected and no public project page should be published yet.
Purpose

Small enough to control, useful enough to test the system.

The pilot is designed to test the Foundation's complete humanitarian workflow: documented need, project approval, partner due diligence, compliance review, financial control, distribution evidence, complaints handling and public completion reporting.

The geographic location and beneficiary target remain intentionally open until a credible needs and market assessment is available.

Eligibility

Need must be documented consistently.

Priority factorsAcute food insecurity, insufficient winter protection, displacement, disability, older-person vulnerability, child-headed or otherwise high-risk household circumstances, subject to local validation.
ExclusionsNo selection based on personal relationship, political influence, sectarian affiliation, payment, favouritism or publicity value.
PrivacyUse household reference codes and collect only the personal information necessary for assessment and delivery.
ReviewSelection method and sample records should be independently reviewed before distribution.
Monitoring

Minimum pilot indicators.

ReachNumber of verified households receiving approved assistance; final target set only after market and budget assessment.
Delivery integrityPercentage of distributions supported by complete distribution records and reconciliation.
Financial controlVerified expenditure versus approved budget, with all material variances explained.
SafeguardingNumber and status of safeguarding or protection concerns; serious incidents escalated immediately.
ComplaintsComplaints received, response status and lessons without publishing complainant identities.
EvidenceRequired financial, partner and delivery evidence uploaded and reviewed before project closure.
Decision gates

Nothing moves automatically.

Gate 1 — Legal & governance readinessFoundation structure, bank arrangements, responsible persons and applicable fundraising/compliance requirements confirmed.
Gate 2 — Location & needs assessmentSpecific location, market prices, beneficiary criteria and security/access risks documented.
Gate 3 — Partner & compliance approvalPartner due diligence, sanctions checks, safeguarding expectations and written agreement complete.
Gate 4 — Funding approvalFunding source and grant restrictions confirmed; budget and any salary allocation approved independently.
Gate 5 — Delivery authorisationDistribution plan, payment controls, evidence requirements and complaints channel confirmed.
Gate 6 — Close & publishTransactions reconciled, evidence reviewed, risks/incidents closed or carried forward, and privacy-safe public report approved.
