Governance policies

Standards for money, people and decision-making.

These documents form the first draft policy framework for the proposed HSKRC Humanitarian Foundation. They are designed to support accountable practice before public fundraising or humanitarian delivery begins.

Status: DraftVersion: 1.0Prepared: October 2026
Financial PolicyBudgeting, approvals, payments, records, restricted funds and financial reporting. Safeguarding PolicyProtection, dignity, consent, privacy, reporting concerns and partner expectations. Conflict of Interest PolicyDisclosure, recusal, related-party decisions, gifts and conflict registers.
Status

Drafts, not legal claims.

These policies are internal governance drafts for a pre-launch initiative. They do not by themselves create charitable registration, tax-deductible status, fundraising authority, employment arrangements or any other legal status.

Before formal adoption: the policies should be reviewed against the legal structure ultimately chosen for the foundation and adjusted to match its actual responsible persons, banking arrangements, reporting obligations and program risks.
