Requires external action or your decision

YOUR DECISION
Choose legal structure
The governing document and registration route depend on the legal structure selected for the Foundation.
PEOPLE
Confirm responsible persons / governing body
Real names, eligibility, consent and formal appointments cannot be created from the website alone.
REGISTRATION
Complete relevant Australian registrations and fundraising compliance
Must be completed with the relevant authorities based on the final structure and activities.
BANKING
Open a separate Foundation bank account
Requires the final legal entity and authorised persons.
DNS
Connect foundation.hskrc.org
Requires access to the DNS/Cloudflare account and verification of HTTPS after the record is created.
PARTNERS
Verify first local partner or office arrangement
Requires actual identities, documents, agreements and risk review.
PROJECT
Approve the first real humanitarian project
Needs a real need assessment, location, beneficiaries, budget, delivery method and approval decision.
FUNDRAISING
Activate donations only after compliance is confirmed
Donation buttons and payment methods should remain disabled until the required status and controls are verified.
