Financial transparency

Separate funds. Documented approvals. Verifiable reporting.

The Foundation's financial model is designed to keep humanitarian funds separate from personal and unrelated organisational finances, with project-level budgets and traceable approvals.

Current position

No public fundraising yet.

During pre-launch, the website is not accepting public donations. Financial reports will only show verified figures from actual Foundation activity after the necessary legal, banking and governance arrangements are completed.

Important: zero figures or demo records must not be presented as real impact, fundraising or expenditure.
Financial reporting model

What future public reporting should show.

IncomeVerified donations, grants and other permitted funding received by the Foundation.
Restricted fundsFunds assigned to a specific purpose tracked separately from unrestricted operating funds.
Program expenditureAmounts spent directly on approved humanitarian projects and eligible program delivery costs.
AdministrationReasonable governance, compliance, banking, technology and staff costs clearly identified.
BalancesUnspent project and operating balances reconciled to Foundation records.
