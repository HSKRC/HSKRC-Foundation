Operational templates

From project idea to documented completion.

Fourteen draft tools translate the foundation’s governance policies into repeatable project, finance, partner and governance workflows. The forms work in the browser and can be printed or saved as PDF. Entries are not submitted to HSKRC or stored by these pages.

Pre-launch: these templates support planning and governance only. They do not authorise fundraising, expenditure or project delivery.
