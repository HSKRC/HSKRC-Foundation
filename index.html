Our mission

Help where it matters — and show how every project is delivered.

Our aim is to support vulnerable communities in Afghanistan through practical, carefully scoped humanitarian projects. We are building the organisation around transparent budgeting, documented delivery and responsible governance from the beginning.

HSKRC Humanitarian Foundation is intended to operate separately from HSKRC's research activities, with its own humanitarian governance, financial records and project reporting.

Transparency framework

Trust should be visible.

Our website is designed to publish project evidence without exposing the private details of vulnerable beneficiaries.

1

Project proposal

Define the need, target group, budget, delivery method and safeguards before fundraising.

2

Controlled funding

Keep humanitarian funds separated from personal and research finances, with documented transactions.

3

Verified delivery

Maintain receipts, distribution records and appropriate evidence of delivery.

4

Public report

Publish a plain-language summary of money received, costs, delivery and outcomes.

About the initiative

Humanitarian work under the HSKRC family of initiatives.

HSKRC Humanitarian Foundation is being developed as a distinct humanitarian initiative connected by name and values to the Hassan Sabbah Knowledge & Research Centre.

The humanitarian foundation will not present itself as a registered charity until registration is formally completed. Legal status, donation eligibility and any tax-deductibility information will be published only after official confirmation.
