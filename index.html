Our mission

Help where it matters — and document how it was delivered.

Our mission is to support vulnerable communities in Afghanistan through practical, carefully scoped humanitarian projects that prioritise dignity, accountability and responsible stewardship.

The humanitarian foundation is intended to operate separately from HSKRC's research activities, with dedicated governance, financial records and project reporting.

Pre-launch commitment We will not present the organisation as a registered charity, accept public donations, or claim tax-deductibility until the relevant registration and compliance requirements are formally completed.

Transparency framework

Trust should be visible.

Project evidence should be public enough to verify delivery while protecting the privacy and dignity of beneficiaries.

1

Project design

Define the need, target group, budget, safeguards and delivery method before fundraising.

2

Controlled funds

Separate humanitarian funds from personal and research finances with documented transactions.

3

Verified delivery

Maintain receipts, distribution records and appropriate evidence of project completion.

4

Public reporting

Publish a clear summary of funds received, costs, delivery and measurable outcomes.

Governance

Good intentions need good systems.

Responsible humanitarian work requires clear roles, records, financial controls and compliance from the beginning.

Organisational safeguards

The foundation is being designed around clear financial separation and documented decision-making.

✓Separate humanitarian banking and financial records
✓Documented project approvals and budgets
✓Partner and transaction due diligence
✓Conflict-of-interest and record-keeping processes

Beneficiary protection

Transparency must never require unnecessary exposure of vulnerable people.

✓Privacy-conscious evidence and reporting
✓Respectful use of photographs and personal information
✓Clear eligibility and distribution records
✓Safeguards appropriate to each project
