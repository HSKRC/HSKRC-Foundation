Our mission

Improving lives through accountable humanitarian action.

HSKRC Humanitarian Foundation is being developed as an Australia-based humanitarian initiative focused on practical support for vulnerable communities in Afghanistan.

Our approach is designed around responsible stewardship, clear project budgets, documented delivery and public reporting.

Pre-launch: the foundation will not present itself as a registered charity, accept public donations, or claim tax-deductibility until the relevant legal and compliance requirements are formally completed.
